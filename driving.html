Driving in South Korea

A Comprehensive Guide for Foreign Residents (2026 Edition)

Introduction

Navigating the South Korean driver's license system is highly organized. This guide covers cars and the specific course requirements for motorcycles.

Special Section: Motorcycle License (2nd Class Small)

The motorcycle skills test is famous for being difficult. It consists of four main obstacles that must be completed without touching the lines or putting your feet down.

Figure 1: Illustration of the Motorcycle Skill Test Course

The 4 Key Obstacles:

  1. 굴절코스 (Crank/Narrow Turn): A sharp 90-degree turn. This is where most people fail.
  2. 곡선코스 (Curve Course): An S-shaped curve that tests balance.
  3. 직진협로코스 (Narrow Path): Driving straight on a very thin elevated plank.
  4. 연속진로전환코스 (Slalom): Weaving between cones.

Path 1: Exchanging a Foreign License (Cars)

If you have a valid license from your home country, you may be able to exchange it for a Korean Class 2 license.

Required Documents

  • Original Foreign Driver's License
  • Original Passport
  • Alien Registration Card (ARC)
  • Three (3) Passport-sized photos
  • Embassy Certificate or Apostille

Path 2: Obtaining a New License

Step Process
1. Safety Education 1-hour video session (Free)
2. Health Check Vision/Hearing test
3. Written Exam 40 Questions (Available in English)
4. Driving Course Basic skills test on a track
5. Road Test Driving on actual public roads
